How to Spend the Perfect Weekend in Melbourne: Brunch, Footy & Downtime

Published by JC & Howl • 4 Mins Read • Updated 2026

1. Start with a Proper Brew and Brunch

There is no better way to kick off a Saturday morning than strolling down Fitzroy or South Yarra for a flat white and smashed avo. Melburnians take their coffee culture seriously, and finding that quiet local cafe sets the right relaxed tone before the afternoon excitement begins.

2. Afternoon Thrills: Catching the Footy with Mates

As the arvo rolls in, the focus shifts to sport. Whether it’s heading to the MCG for an intense AFL clash or gathering at a local pub to watch the NRL on the big screen, footy is the social glue of the city. We catch up over a cold drink, dissect the team lineups, and share the buzz of every goal and tackle together.

3. Winding Down with Casual Digital Games

After the match ends and the energy cools, Sunday evenings are all about winding down at home. Many locals enjoy switching on their consoles or tablets for some casual digital gaming. From sports simulation apps like AFL Evolution to strategy and trivia puzzles, it’s a fun, low-key way to unwind before the Monday workweek kicks off.

4. The Balance of City Living

Great food, thrilling live sport, and relaxing digital downtime at home—that is the beauty of Melbourne living. It’s all about balance and enjoying the simple moments with good company.
